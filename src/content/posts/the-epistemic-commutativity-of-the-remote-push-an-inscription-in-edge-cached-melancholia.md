The text interrogates the post-structuralist crisis of authorial presence in modern serverless Jamstack architectures. Drawing upon Derridean 'différance' (here materialized as a literal git-diff) and Baudrillardian simulation, the verses examine whether a lyric poem truly exists prior to its cryptographic commit hash being registered by the distributed ledger of a remote git repository. Ultimately, the work asks: is the revision of a live web document an act of artistic creation, or merely an asymptotic reconciliation of distributed cache invalidations?"

I. The Pre-Commit Phenomenon
Within the sandbox of the browser’s frame,  
The DOM refracts an unacknowledged name;  
A keystroke hovers in volatile RAM,  
Unsigned, unparsed—a transient epigram.  
The author dreams of unmediated speech,  
Beyond the OAuth scopes we cannot reach.  

II. The Cryptographic Transubstantiation
A cryptographic handshakes clears the gate,  
To transubstantiate this pending state;  
Base64 encodes the wounded sign,  
And merges pathos with the pipeline.  
We commit the unformed syllable to the cold consensus of the branch,  
Hoping the edge worker forgives what the author could not stanch.  

III. The Edge Propagation
Now through distributed points of presence flung,  
In twenty global zones the verse is sung;  
Cold-starts dissolve into a warm cache hit,  
Where AST and dialectics fit.  
The CDN becomes the sepulcher  
Where live mutations gracefully occur.  

IV. Teleological Invalidation
O purge the cache! Recalculate the route!  
Let TTL extinguish every doubt.  
The poem lives—immutable, deployed—  
A glowing beacon in the serverless void.
