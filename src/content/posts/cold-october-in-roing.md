Experiencing the cold morning breeze in Roing 
   -Siamsiam Pa 
 
  At six-thirty this morning, 
  a gentle breeze painted my face, 
  slipped quietly through my chest 
  and travelled down my legs. 
 
  I could not withstand its cold, 
  I pulled a thin blanket over my body 
  and lay still, listening 
  to the breath of the morning. 
 
  Not long after, 
  a soft drizzle joined the breeze. 
  Oh, how pleasant the weather was! 
 
  I woke wondering, 
  from where have these cold winds come, 
  so early in October? 
 
  Freshened up, 
  I returned to my reading— 
  George Orwell’s 1984, 
  a dystopian world 
  speaking of a coming English socialism. 
 
  But I could not bring myself 
  to mingle that prophetic darkness 
  with the gentle chill of the morning. 
 
  So I halted my reading 
  and stared through the window. 
 
  Clouds had descended upon the mountains, 
  covering their familiar faces. 
  I breathed in the fresh, cold air, 
  and somewhere deep within me, 
  a quiet voice spoke: 
  Oh! What a lovely day it was.
