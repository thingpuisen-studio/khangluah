Siamsiam’s report came out today.  
An A+, a joy to his teachers,  
His mother, and me.  
Ma’am Sentei-i says,  
“He has improved so much.”

Yet his little report  
Took me back to my own.  
My Preparatory days,  
When I had to repeat a class.

But those old reports  
Never wrote my destiny;  
Nor will this A+  
Write my son’s future.

Grades may mark a moment,  
But not the journey.  
Only time and the Divine Being  
Know what tomorrow holds.
