Beneath the starry dome of winter cold,
The silent hills a deeper secret hold.
When earthly voices fade into the dark,
The soul beholds an everlasting spark.

Far from the crowded streets and hurried days,
The heavens shine with wonder and with praise.
In quiet rest my heart is still and free,
A gentle peace that watches over me.

## Silence and Faith

In the stillness of the mountain night, prayer ceases to be a list of requests and becomes an encounter with wonder, silence, and grace.
